Audit date: 2026-09-29. Source revision: `45f9683`. Scope: read-only source, assets, configuration, and existing automated checks. ## Executive decision

**Not ready to call finished yet.** The three-stage arcade loop, Show Me, boss progression, retry, and replay are connected, and the existing tests pass. The release risk is concentrated in a small number of visible inconsistencies: saved achievements appear locked, Stats is disconnected from the stats actually written, Account presents nonfunctional success flows, one achievement cannot be earned, a fresh player can miss how to move, a live Exit can discard a run, and terminal music does not transition. Fix or hide these; do not add a larger online product to make the existing menu seem complete.

The one **P0 verification gate** is a clean production iOS launch: an eagerly imported Firebase Auth initialization requires an API key that is not in source-controlled release configuration. A remote EAS environment may supply it, so a crash in the existing distributed build is **not established**. A clean build without it would fail before the app renders. Confirm the production/TestFlight artifact or remove that eager dependency before submission.

### Show Me: current implementation and teaching quality

`src/scenes/showMeDemoTimeline.js:1-13,51-116` is a **27-second deterministic presentation storyboard**, not the production collision/spawn/scoring session. `src/scenes/ShowMeDemo.js:5-8,31-46,61-96` uses the production Canvas renderers for ships, bullets, enemies, powerups and boss, so it visually resembles play without writing real score, achievements, or stats. The player can leave immediately, skip, replay, or start a real session. Its interval and AppState listener are cleaned on unmount; inactive app state stops timeline progress (`ShowMeDemo.js:35-49`). Field size derives from window dimensions and insets, with lower bounds (`:14-21`), but small-height/large-text rendering still needs a screenshot (R14).

Its eight steps demonstrate tilt movement and the Pause route to touch drag; hold-to-fire versus Auto-Fire; lining up straight shots; +100 then +125 combo points; a guaranteed **practice-only** shield drop versus random real drops; a one-hit/four-second block; visible boss HP/telegraph/volley/dodge; and three-stage victory (`showMeDemoTimeline.js:4-13,64-116`). It does not reproduce live Phase-2 flight choreography or full boss attack cadence, and it has no own firing/impact audio calls; menu music may continue from its parent. Those are acceptable simplifications for a short primer, not reasons to rebuild it. **Yes: it teaches enough for a new player to understand the basic loop**, especially the distinction between points, shield drops, normal-kill counter, and boss HP. Preserve this feature unless a device test reveals a concrete readability/audio problem.

## HUD, audio, comfort, and layout

The current HUD already condenses score/stage/lives into a top row and level/enemy progress plus shield into a second row; boss fights replace the progress copy with `BOSS FIGHT · Hit the ship`, and boss HP sits in its own bar (`src/components/GameHUD.js:10-48`; `src/scenes/GameScreen.js:191-210,250-266`). That directly addresses the earlier confusion about shooting a boss while seeing `0/8`. Score uses grouping; lives use a labeled heart/count; shield has text rather than color alone. A separate BonusBanner and FireButton `AUTO ON/MANUAL` badge exist (`GameScreen.js:258-300`; `src/components/FireButton.js:28-67`). The old user screenshot should not be treated as proof that this current HUD overlaps. Its measured HUD height and boss-bar fallback reduce collision risk (`GameScreen.js:191-207,274-282`); R14 lists what still needs a device image.

Pause has Resume first, 44-point rows/steppers, session controls, audio controls, and Quit to Menu in a scrollable overlay (`src/components/PauseOverlay.js:9-137,148-211`). It is usable in source and has an overflow escape; the direct HUD Exit, not Pause, is the dangerous quit route. Audio cues for firing, impacts, drops, boss events, levels, and terminal effects are wired (`src/engine/gameEventEffects.js:43-94`). Menu music is intentionally attenuated to 25% of the user's saved level (`src/engine/audio.js:69-73`), so the former loud-menu complaint should be rechecked by ear, not assumed unresolved. Pause leaves music playing rather than pausing it; this is a policy to test, not a confirmed defect. Backgrounding pauses gameplay and requires explicit Resume (`GameScreen.js:129-134`); audio interruption/background behavior still needs device verification.

The controls do provide a large labeled Fire target and accessibility activation (`src/components/FireButton.js:28-59`), left/right placement, and a non-tilt touch mode (`src/hooks/usePlayerControls.js:85-100`). The notable comfort gaps are discoverability of that mode (R06), silent-switch/motion policy (R17), top toast safe area and fixed-width completion/demo text (R14). Avoid a wholesale visual redesign: the upgraded player/enemy/boss visuals, dark neon backgrounds, and stage-specific boss identities are coherent in source; the menu title/emoji, Stats placeholder art, and generic victory emoji are the remaining brand seams, with Stats/victory already covered by R03/R13. The configured opaque 1024×1024 icon matches the JS splash icon; `assets/splash.png` is an unused white placeholder, **not** a player-visible launch image.
